Merit Badge Resources:
Steps to Earn a Merit Badge:
Choose a badge of interest.
Review requirements.
Find a counselor (Scoutmaster approves blue cards).
Complete requirements with counselor guidance.
FAQs:
What is a merit badge?: An achievement for completing BSA requirements on a subject (130+ available).
Partials: Incomplete requirements signed off for future completion.
Blue Card: Tracks progress; obtain from Scoutmaster.
Pamphlets: Borrow from troop librarian or buy at a Scout store.
Worksheets: BSA-provided tools to organize requirements.
Cyber Chip Steps:
Print workbook: Scout BSA Cyber Chip Workbook.
Complete grade-specific requirements.
Get approval from ASM for Cyber Chip card.
